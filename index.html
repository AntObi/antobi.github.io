    Machine Learning Scientist · Materials Science · Scientific Computing

    Anthony Onwuli

    I build machine-learning models and scientific software for materials discovery. My work spans atomistic modelling, chemical representations, high-throughput simulation, and the infrastructure needed to turn research models into reliable scientific workflows.

    Selected work Publications GitHub

    Current focus

    Machine learning for materials discovery

    I am an ML Scientist II at Materials Nexus. I work across model development, atomistic simulation, data generation, evaluation, and scientific ML infrastructure. The common thread is making computational materials workflows more useful, reproducible, and easier to extend.

    Atomistic ML

    Models for atomistic simulation

    Machine-learning interatomic potentials, property models, and the training/evaluation systems around them. I am particularly interested in models that preserve useful physical structure while remaining practical inside larger simulation workflows.

    Scientific systems

    Research models that survive contact with workflows

    High-throughput simulation, dataset preparation, model interfaces, reproducibility, and the engineering needed to connect research code to repeatable computational campaigns.

    Representations

    Chemical information for machine learning

    My academic work studied how elements and ionic species can be represented for materials informatics, including learned embeddings and their relationship to chemical similarity.

    Materials design

    From chemical space to candidate materials

    I have worked on high-throughput screening, chemical-space mapping, generative materials models, and chemically informed constraints for navigating large candidate spaces.

    Background
